# The Cagayan River Basin Corridor

> **Cagayan de Oro Watershed Resilience & Native Stingless Bee Agro-Canopy Report**  
> An interactive editorial investigation into Northern Mindanao's riparian revegetation, native kiwot bee sanctuaries, and monsoon flood buffering from Bukidnon to Macajalar Bay.

---

## 🌿 Overview

The **Cagayan River Basin Corridor Initiative** models the ecological restoration of the 52-kilometer Cagayan de Oro River waterway. 

By integrating community agro-forestry, engineered giant bamboo (*Dendrocalamus asper*) riparian buffers, and native stingless bee (*Tetragonula biroi* / *Kiwot*) sanctuaries across key river barangays (Macasandig, Balulang, Carmen, Malasag, and Bonbon), this platform provides civic planners with an interactive telemetry model to evaluate monsoon surge attenuation and community agro-forestry dividends.

---

## ✨ Key Capabilities & Interactive Visuals

1. **Interactive River Basin Corridor Map**:
   - Vector cartography depicting the north-flowing Cagayan River from the Bukidnon foothills through dramatic limestone riverbends into Macajalar Bay.
   - Clickable barangay nodes revealing active colony counts, seasonal honey yields, and dominant botanical canopies (Lauan, Molave, Giant Bamboo, Vetiver, and Coastal Mangroves).
   - Filter controls to isolate the *Upland Gorge Reach* vs. *Estuary & Bay*.

2. **Riparian Flood Buffer Analysis**:
   - Editorial reporting on nature-based engineering solutions that reduce flash flood velocity by 34% along vulnerable riverbends.

3. **Watershed Resilience Impact Estimator**:
   - Parametric simulation engine allowing users to adjust riverbank revegetation zones (5,000 m² to 100,000 m²) and stingless bee colony density.
   - Select between three agro-botanical zones: *Bamboo Riparian*, *Coffee & Cacao Agro-Forest*, and *Estuary Mangrove*.
   - Dynamically calculates raw medicinal honey yield (kg), native pollinator populations, monsoon absorption capacity (liters), and river-warden family livelihood support.
